## Editorial summary

So as to reach the Soil Deal for Europe goal of having 75% of EU soils healthy or significantly improved by 2030, an access to reliable and harmonised data and knowledge is required. 

The SoilWise catalogue (https://repository.soilwise-he.eu/) aims at providing a single access point for scattered data and knowledge in Europe, making them FAIR (Findable, Accessible, Interoperable, Reusable). A such goal is realized during the publication step by providers, on a source repository harvested by SoilWise. 

This first version of a practical guide displays how to make FAIR data and knowledge discoverable on SoilWise. It considers the use of Zenodo source repository and emphasizes the observational data and websites specificities.
